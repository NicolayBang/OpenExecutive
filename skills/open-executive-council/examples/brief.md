# Decision: hire now or pilot onboarding improvements first

This is fictional test data, not a real company's finances.

Company: a bootstrapped SaaS business with two founders. The founders want a
profitable small company, not venture funding. Current unrestricted cash is
$120,000. Monthly collected revenue is $20,000; monthly cash expenses are
$30,000, including founder pay. An additional employee would cost $6,000 per
month all-in. A $40,000 invoice is outstanding and has not been collected;
do not count it as cash. Assume no growth or other changes for runway math.

Customers struggle to finish onboarding. Ten recent support conversations
mention setup confusion, but we have no activation baseline and no evidence
that another feature would fix it. A founder can run a two-week onboarding
pilot with five existing customers for $1,000 total.

Question: should we hire now or run the pilot first? Ask finance to independently
calculate current runway and runway with the hire, and product to assess the
evidence and propose a measurable pilot. Preserve disagreement and uncertainty.
